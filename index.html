Questions

Good to know.

Do I have to pay every month?

No. The build is a one-time fee and hosting is free on the setups I use. Your only running cost is the domain name, about RM50–150 a year. The care plan is optional.

Can I change the menu myself?

Yes. All the menu items and prices sit in one file, and the handover guide shows you how to edit it. If you’d rather not, the care plan covers it.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, your current menu (a PDF or photos are fine), good food photos, opening hours, outlet addresses and your WhatsApp and delivery links. I’ll send a checklist.

Do you take the food photos?

I work with the photos you have. If they need help, I can suggest a food photographer, priced separately.

Can you put me on Google Maps?

I’ll link the site to your Google Business Profile and help you check it’s filled in. The profile itself has to be claimed by the owner.

How do payments work?

50% deposit to start, 50% before launch. Bank transfer or DuitNow. Everything is written down in a short agreement before we begin.
