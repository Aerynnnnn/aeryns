Good to know.

Do I have to pay every month?

No. The build is a one-time fee and hosting is free on the setups I use. Your only running cost is the domain name, about RM50–150 a year. The care plan is optional.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Small brands of all kinds: shops and online stores, studios and gyms, clinics and salons, makers and crafts, agencies, cafés and restaurants. If your customers find you on their phone, I can help.

Can I change prices and products myself?

Yes. Products, prices, menus and schedules sit in one file, and the handover guide shows you how to edit it. If you’d rather not, the care plan covers it.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, your product or price list (a PDF or photos are fine), good photos, opening hours, addresses and your WhatsApp, booking or shop links. I’ll send a checklist.

Do you take the photos?

I work with the photos you have. If they need help, I can suggest a photographer, priced separately.

Can you build a full online shop with payments?

I build product catalogues that send orders to WhatsApp, Shopee or your existing store. Full checkout with card payments is quoted separately, since it needs a payment provider in your name.

Can you put me on Google Maps?

I’ll link the site to your Google Business Profile and help you check it’s filled in. The profile itself has to be claimed by the owner.

How do payments work?

50% deposit to start, 50% before launch. Bank transfer or DuitNow. Everything is written down in a short agreement before we begin.
