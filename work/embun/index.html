Why "Embun"

Embun is the Malay word for morning dew.

At six in the morning the tea terraces above Brinchang are soaked in it. That's when we cut the pandan and pick up the ginger, and by the afternoon it's in the pot.

Two of us, one steam kettle and a shophouse workshop on Jalan Besar. We pour 200 bottles at a time, write the batch number on every label, and only pour again when the last batch is nearly gone.

The ingredient story

Four plants, all from Pahang

Every formula is built around one of them. Short ingredient lists you can read, and no fragrance but the plant's own.

01 / 04

Halia

Bentong ginger

Fibrous, fiery and famous across Malaysia. We slice the root thin and leave it in oil for three weeks, which pulls out the warming, brightening part without the sting.

Grown
A family farm in Bentong, 90 minutes down the hill
Does
Brightens dull skin, evens tone
Find it in
Halia Glow Serum
02 / 04

Pandan

Screwpine leaf

The smell of every kuih stall. It grows along our neighbour's fence in Ringlet; we cut it the morning we pour, so the balm smells the way the leaf does.

Grown
Ringlet, 20 minutes from the studio
Does
Soothes and calms, rich in antioxidants
Find it in
Pandan Night Balm, Embun Dew Cream
03 / 04

Teh hijau

Highland green tea

Young leaves from a small estate above Brinchang, steamed and dried within the day. Brewed strong, it becomes the base of the toner instead of plain water.

Grown
Brinchang, at 1,500 m
Does
Calms redness, helps with shine
Find it in
Teh Hijau Toner, Embun Dew Cream
04 / 04

Bunga raya

Hibiscus, the national flower

Its petals carry gentle fruit acids. We dry them on racks in the studio for a week, then infuse them into oil and soap: a soft polish, never a scrub.

Grown
Flower farms in Kuala Terla
Does
Gently lifts dead skin, adds glow
Find it in
Bunga Raya Cleansing Oil, Bunga Raya Body Wash

Warm it between your fingers first. It spreads further.

The routine

Three steps, about two minutes.

Made for skin that lives in 30 °C and 80% humidity: light layers that sink in, nothing that sits on top.

  1. Cleanse

    AM & PM · 60 sec

    Massage Bunga Raya Cleansing Oil into dry skin, add a splash of water until it turns milky, then rinse.

  2. Treat

    AM & PM · 20 sec

    Press two drops of Halia Glow Serum into damp skin. Follow with the toner on hot days.

  3. Seal

    PM · 30 sec

    Warm a pea of Pandan Night Balm between your fingers and press it over cheeks and any dry patches.
