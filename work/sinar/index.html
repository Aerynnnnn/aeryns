Your first visit

Nervous? Here's exactly what happens

About 45 minutes from the front door to the front door. You can stop at any point.

  1. 1

    Book, and tell us how you feel

    Tick "I'm nervous" when you book. We give you a longer slot and the first chair by the window.

  2. 2

    Arrive ten minutes early

    A short health form on a tablet, a glass of cold water, and no drill sounds in the waiting area.

  3. 3

    Talk first, chair upright

    Your dentist asks what's bothering you and what has gone badly before. Nothing starts until you say so.

  4. 4

    A gentle look, on screen

    Raise your left hand and we stop. Photos of your teeth go up on the monitor so you can see what we see.

  5. 5

    A written plan and price

    You leave with a printed quote. Book the next step at the desk or take it home and think about it.

Kids' dentistry

Their first dentist should be a good memory

We see children from their first tooth or first birthday. Short visits, a parent in the room, and nothing done without a "tell, show, do" first.

  • "Meet the chair" visit, free. A ride up and down, counting teeth with a mirror, a sticker at the end.
  • Saturday mornings and school holidays are kept for families.
  • Sealants and fluoride to protect new adult teeth, explained to you and to them.
  • Brushing coaching with your child's own toothbrush. Bring it along.

Questions

Before you come in

Something else? .

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, if a chair is free, and we always squeeze in toothaches. Booking ahead means you won't wait.

Will you push extra treatments on me?

No. You get a written list of what we found, what is urgent and what can wait, with prices. You choose what to do and when.

Can I use my company's dental panel?

Most likely. Tell us your company when you book and we'll check your coverage before the visit.

Does a scale and polish hurt?

It can feel scratchy and a little sensitive, especially if it's been a while. We can numb sensitive areas, and you can raise your hand for a break at any time.

How early should my child come for a first visit?

When the first tooth appears, or by their first birthday. Early visits are short and mostly about getting used to the place.

Can I come after work?

Yes. We're open until 9pm Monday to Friday; the last appointment starts at 8:30pm.

What if I need to cancel?

Let us know a day before if you can. There's no cancellation fee; we'd just like to offer the slot to someone in pain.
