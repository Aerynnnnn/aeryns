Why eight

Eight reformers. One coach. Nobody hiding at the back.

Eight is the number one coach can actually watch. It's small enough for a hand on your shoulder when your ribs flare, and big enough that the room still has energy at 6:30am.

Every class on the timetable shows those eight places, so you can see how full it is before you book.

Your first visit

What happens on day one

  1. 01

    Pick a class

    Any class works. If you've never been on a reformer, the ones marked Foundations go slower and explain more.

  2. 02

    Arrive 15 minutes early

    Level 2, lift lobby B. We'll show you the lockers and showers and fill in a one-page health form with you.

  3. 03

    Tell your coach anything

    Sore knee, recent baby, desk job, nervous: they'd rather know. Every move has an easier and a harder version.

  4. 04

    Train

    Your coach sets up your machine or weights and talks you through it. Rest whenever you need to; nobody's counting.

  5. 05

    Have a plan before you leave

    Five minutes after class to agree what to book next. No sales pitch, just a sensible next step.

Bring: grip socks for reformer (or buy a pair for RM15), flat trainers for strength, a water bottle. Towels, mats, shampoo and a hairdryer are here.

Questions

Before you book

I've never done pilates or lifted weights. Is that OK?

Yes, most people start that way. Book a Foundations reformer or Kettlebell Basics class and tell your coach it's your first time. Every move has an easier version.

What should I wear?

Anything you can move in. Grip socks for reformer (RM15 at the desk), flat trainers for strength, bare feet or socks for mobility.

What if I'm late?

Doors close 5 minutes after the start, for your safety: the warm-up matters. If you miss it we'll move you to the next class with space, no charge.

How does the waitlist work?

Join it from the timetable. If someone cancels, the first person on the list gets the spot automatically and a text, up to 2 hours before class. No spot, no charge.

Can I come if I'm pregnant or have an injury?

Often yes, but check with your doctor first and tell us before you book so we can put you with the right coach and class. A 1:1 first is sometimes the better start.

Where do I park?

In the building's basement. Bring your ticket to the desk and we'll validate the first hour. It's also a 4-minute walk from LRT Kerinchi, exit B.

Can I freeze or cancel my monthly pass?

Pause it for up to 4 weeks a year, or cancel with 14 days' notice. No lock-in and no joining fee.
