The pieces

Six weeks,
one maker.

  1. Find the timber

    Beams from old shophouses and kampung homes, dried slowly for a year.

  2. Cut and join

    Mortise and tenon, dovetails and wooden pegs. No screws, no metal brackets.

  3. Oil and finish

    Three coats of natural oil, hand-rubbed. Safe for food, kind to skin.

  4. Sign and deliver

    Each piece is signed by its maker and delivered by our own team.
